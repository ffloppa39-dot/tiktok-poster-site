DraftBox

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

DraftBox is a personal tool used by its owner to upload the owner's videos to TikTok drafts. It does not operate a server that stores TikTok credentials or video files.

Information used

To connect to TikTok and upload a video, DraftBox uses the owner's TikTok authorization code and access and refresh tokens. It also reads the video file selected by the owner for upload.

Where information is stored

The owner stores credentials locally in the private DraftBox folder on the owner's device. DraftBox does not send credentials to unrelated third parties, sell personal information, or maintain a separate online database. Credentials are sent to TikTok only as needed to authorize and make the owner's requested upload to TikTok.

Permission requested

DraftBox requests the TikTok video.upload permission. This allows the owner to upload videos to TikTok drafts for review and manual publication in the TikTok app.

Revoking access and deleting local data

You can revoke DraftBox's access in TikTok: open Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps, select DraftBox, and remove its access. You can delete the local credentials by removing the private tokens.json file and any local .env file used by the scripts.

Website hosting

These information pages are static. They do not use analytics or advertising scripts. The hosting provider may process basic technical request data to deliver the pages under its own policies.
